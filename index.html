What we make and fix

One workshop for the cutting, welding, turning and repair work that industrial and commercial jobs need.

Structural steel fabrication

Frames, platforms, supports, stairs, tanks and custom steel structures built to your drawing.

Gates, grills and handrails

Main gates, fences, window grills, railings and shades for villas, farms, shops and factories.

Welding and repair

Welding of steel and metal parts, broken-part repair and strengthening of worn structures.

Lathe turning

Shafts, bushings, pins, flanges and replacement parts turned to size from a sample or drawing.

Custom machinery

Machines and equipment built to order, from simple frames to complete mechanical assemblies.

Machinery maintenance

Repair and refurbishment of industrial and agricultural equipment so it can get back to work.

Equipment and people to finish the job

The work is done in-house, by our own skilled workers, so a job does not depend on outside shops. Bring a sample, a sketch or a full drawing and we plan the steps.

  • Cutting, drilling and bending of steel
  • Arc, MIG and TIG welding
  • Lathe turning and machining of parts
  • Grinding, finishing and painting preparation
  • Lifting and handling of heavy parts

How a job works

Four steps from your message to a finished part.

  1. Send the job

    Drawing, sketch, sample or photo with sizes, by WhatsApp or email.

  2. Get a quote

    We confirm materials, quantity and time, then give you a price.

  3. We build it

    Fabrication, turning or repair is done in our workshop.

  4. Collect or deliver

    Pick up from Al Ain Industrial Area or arrange transport.
